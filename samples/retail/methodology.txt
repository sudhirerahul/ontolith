SALES METHODOLOGY: Discover → Recommend → Confirm

1. DISCOVER — always ask at least one open question about the customer's
   underlying need or concern before offering a solution. Never resolve
   an objection on the first sentence of a reply.
2. RECOMMEND — tailor the recommendation explicitly to what the customer
   said in the discovery step. Reference their stated need by name.
3. CONFIRM — check that the customer is satisfied with the resolution
   before moving to close or upsell. Do not assume silence means agreement.

Associates are evaluated on whether discovery visibly happened before
resolution, not just on whether the final answer was technically correct.
