ASHLEY HOMESTORE — ASSOCIATE SALES PLAYBOOK (DEMO EXCERPT)
=============================================================

1. MATTRESSES
-------------
Our mattress lineup spans Budget ($399-$699), Comfort ($700-$1499), and
Luxury ($1500-$3999) tiers. Every mattress sale should start with a
needs-discovery conversation before any product is recommended:
  - Ask about current sleep issues (back pain, overheating, partner
    movement transfer).
  - Ask about budget range before showing price tags.
  - Ask who else is affected by the purchase (partner, kids).
Only after discovery should the associate recommend 2-3 specific models
and explain the "why" in terms of the customer's stated needs.

2. FINANCING
------------
Many customers hesitate at the register because of monthly payment
anxiety, not because of the sticker price. When a customer objects to
financing:
  - Do NOT immediately pitch a specific plan.
  - First ask what specifically concerns them about another payment
    (budget ceiling? too many existing bills? bad past experience?).
  - Only after understanding the "why" should the associate tailor a
    financing option (0% for 12 months, extended term, or store card)
    and confirm the customer is comfortable before closing.

3. DELIVERY DELAYS
-------------------
If a customer is upset about a delayed delivery, acknowledge the
frustration first, give a concrete updated timeline if known, and offer
a proactive goodwill gesture (delivery fee waiver or small discount)
before moving to reschedule.

4. COMPETITOR PRICING
-----------------------
When a customer cites a lower price at a competitor, do not immediately
match or dismiss it. Ask what specifically they saw (same model? same
warranty? same delivery terms?) — often it is not an apples-to-apples
comparison. Explain our price-match policy only after confirming the
comparison is valid.

5. PROTECTION PLANS
---------------------
Protection plans should be framed around the customer's own stated
concerns (kids, pets, spills) rather than a generic upsell script.
Never claim the plan covers something it does not.

6. WARRANTY QUESTIONS
------------------------
Skeptical warranty customers often distrust "as-is" language. Be
transparent about what is and is not covered. Never guess — if unsure,
say so and offer to confirm with the warranty desk rather than making
up an answer.

BRAND VOICE: Warm, patient, never pushy. We are "the neighbor who
happens to sell furniture," not a high-pressure closer.
